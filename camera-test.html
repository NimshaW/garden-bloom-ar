Camera Test

First check the webcam independently of the AR library. When the webcam works here, use Stop Camera & Open AR to scan the Hiro marker.

Checking site security...

Click "Test Webcam". When Chrome asks to use the camera, choose Allow.

If camera is blocked

  1. Make sure the address is http://127.0.0.1:5500/camera-test.html (or the VS Code Live Server localhost address), not file:///.
  2. Chrome: click the site settings icon beside the address bar, then set Camera → Allow and refresh.
  3. Windows: Settings → Privacy & security → Camera. Turn on Camera access, Let apps access your camera, and Let desktop apps access your camera (where available).
  4. Close other camera tabs, Zoom, Teams, Discord and other apps using the webcam. Check the laptop camera shutter/switch.
  5. If camera works here but AR is blank, click Start Garden AR + Camera on index.html. Try the Switch camera mode option or Camera details. Ensure A-Frame/AR.js load over the internet.
